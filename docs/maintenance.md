## Evidence owed

- The Windows entry's `limine.conf` and the loader sealed over it, prepared first and renamed together under held signals, under the real watchers and ESP: checklist 5.1 and 5.4. The order of the two renames is the one the release's run recorded; the preparation, the held signals and the handling of a write error of the ESP are verified by hermetic cases alone.
- The hardware rows that C10's table lists as still owed for every change after the release's record, 0.1.1's included: a full run of stages 0 to 7 on a candidate closes most of them, with checklist 5.4, `omarchy refresh limine` with the entry enabled, for the Windows entry's place. The rows that need firmware holding an entry under another owner, or a boot entry whose name is not valid UTF-16, close with the second machine below, and checklist 5.6's note-only row has no record yet.
- Whether BitLocker stays quiet when Windows is started through a chainload entry alone and the loader is sealed again in between: Microsoft's pages say it cannot (C8), and no machine has a record. The checklist asks for no such start (stage 5 keeps a note-only row); closes with a record from a field report.
- A second machine's record, from another firmware vendor, through [field-testing.md](field-testing.md) or the release checklist.
- The record of a stock single-boot Omarchy install, where the fallback loader and its `/EFI fallback` menu entry exist from the start. The recorded machine (C10) was installed beside Windows and had neither.
- BitLocker on Windows Pro, Enterprise or Education, where an organisation's policy can change the binding.
- The watchers' event schedules on a real systemd: a change that lands after the pass's last proof and before it exits, and a stop at shutdown while the pass waits (spec, D8). No checklist row produces them; closes with a row for each, run on hardware.
- Checklist 3.3's start of each other kernel entry of the menu under Secure Boot: the release's records started `linux-omarchy` alone, although they show the other image signed. Closes with that row's record.
- Stage 7 on firmware that does not expose its built-in defaults as volatile variables, where the rebuild writes `--microsoft` alone; the recorded rebuild used the firmware's defaults (C10). Closes with that stage's record on such firmware.
- The contract workflow's issue on a failed scheduled run, which only a failed scheduled run shows.
- The recovery guide's procedure for a write error of the ESP has no record: no machine has met one. Closes with a record of the procedure on a machine whose ESP reported an error, or of one made to fail on purpose, such as a device-mapper `flakey` target under a scratch ESP.
